Louder Support

Louder is an arcade game for iPhone where you dash on the beat. If something is not working, the answers below cover the usual causes. If they do not help, write to me at ceskew@mac.com.

Louder has loud sound and flashing lights. The flashes button at the top of the screen reduces the flashing. If you are sensitive to flashing lights, please use it or do not play.

How to play

  • Drag anywhere on the screen to steer your ship. It fires by itself on the beat.
  • A ring closes on your ship in time with the kick drum. Tap as it lands to dash through enemies.
  • A tap off the beat gives a short, weak dash.
  • Kill streaks add layers to the music. Getting hit takes them away.
  • Fill the meter at the bottom, then land a dash on the beat to trigger the drop.

Common questions

I can't hear anything

Turn the volume up with the side buttons and check that the button at the top of the screen says sound on. If you are using Bluetooth headphones, check that they are connected.

The ring and the beat don't line up

Open sync from the title screen. Watch the ring and listen to the kick. If the ring closes before you hear the kick, choose later. If it closes after, choose earlier. Bluetooth headphones usually need a value between 150 and 250.

The flashing is too much

Tap flashes on at the top of the screen to switch it to the reduced setting.

The game feels slow or choppy

Close other apps and try again. Louder lowers its own picture quality on older phones to keep the timing accurate.

I watched an ad but did not get my continue

The continue is given when the ad finishes. Closing it early does not count. You can continue once per run. If no ad is available, the button does not appear.

My best score disappeared

Your best score is saved on your phone only. It is erased if you delete the app, and it does not move to a new phone.

How do I stop personalised ads?

Go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking and turn off the switch for Louder. See the Privacy Policy for more.
