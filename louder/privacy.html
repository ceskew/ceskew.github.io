Louder Privacy Policy

Effective 19 November 2026

Louder is an iPhone game made by Christopher Eskew. This policy explains what information is collected when you play it, who collects it, and what you can do about it.

The short version

  • Louder has no accounts and never asks for your name, email address or contacts.
  • Your scores and settings stay on your phone.
  • Louder is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. Google collects some information from your device in order to show and measure those ads.

What the game itself stores

Louder saves three things on your device: your best score, your sound sync setting, and whether you have finished the tutorial. This information never leaves your phone and is erased when you delete the app.

What the advertising partner collects

Louder uses the Google AdMob software kit to show ads between games and to offer an optional ad in exchange for a continue. When ads are requested or shown, Google may collect:

  • Your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location, such as your city or country
  • Device identifiers, including the advertising identifier (IDFA) if you allow tracking
  • Information about the ads you are shown and whether you interact with them
  • How the app is used around the ads, such as when it is opened
  • Diagnostic information, such as crash and performance data from the advertising software

Google uses this information to deliver ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure ad performance, prevent fraud and, where you have allowed it, show ads that are personalised to you. Google acts under its own privacy policy:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

I do not receive or keep a copy of this information in a form that identifies you. I see only combined figures, such as the number of ads shown.

Your choices

  • Tracking permission. The first time you open Louder, iOS asks whether the app may track you. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the advertising identifier is not shared and ads are not personalised using it. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Consent in Europe and the UK. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, Louder shows a consent form before any ad is loaded. Ads are only requested in line with the choices you make there.
  • Personalised ads. You can turn off personalised ads across apps in Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising, and manage Google's ad settings at myadcenter.google.com.
  • Deleting your data. Deleting the app removes everything Louder stored on your phone. For data held by Google, use the links above.

Children

Louder is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact me and I will help.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
